About me

Hi, I’m Natty

UX designer with an industrial design background, based in Bangkok.

I design for a more mindful and inclusive society, one everyday product at a time.

I’m a UX designer with about 3 years of experience in Thai fintech and cloud products. Today I work on Paotang Pay, Krungthai’s everyday-payments product, where I advocate for user-centered solutions that improve customer satisfaction and still line up with business goals.

Being aware of human diversity, I’ve always felt driven to use my skills to help build a more mindful and inclusive society. My focus is social wellness, both physical and mental, and I aim for a design approach that respects individual values, rights and privacy.

I know my own viewpoint carries bias, so I actively seek feedback from diverse perspectives. That habit is why so much of my work leans on research, co-design and testing with real people.
